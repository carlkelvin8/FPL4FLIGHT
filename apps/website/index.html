Features

Everything a Pilot Needs, in One App

From pre-flight planning to post-flight logging — FPL4FLIGHT covers every phase of your flight operations.

✈

ICAO Flight Plans

Create and submit CAAP-compliant ICAO flight plans (ATS 2019-1). Fill out forms digitally with auto-populated aircraft data and export to PDF.

📄

Digital CAAP Forms

Access PNP arrival checklists, pre-flight checklists, passenger manifests, and flight logbooks. All forms match official government templates with pixel-perfect PDF export.

💬

Pilot Community Chat

Connect with fellow pilots in real-time. Share weather reports, coordinate flights, exchange NOTAMs, and collaborate across multiple channels with reactions, replies, voice messages, and image sharing.

🗺

Live Map & Tracking

Full-screen GPS map with real-time position, speed, altitude, and heading. Track flights with background GPS, view track polylines, and access a database of 20+ Philippine airports with ICAO codes.

📋

Pilot Logbook

Comprehensive hour logging — PIC, SIC, Dual, Day, Night, Cross-Country, IFR, instrument approaches, and aircraft category landings. Includes currency tracker per FAR 61.57 rules and PDF export.

⚡

Flight Scheduling

Organize your flights with Today, Tomorrow, and Upcoming sections. Full flight details with route visualization, gate info, PIC assignments, and status tracking.

🔧

Aircraft Management

Maintain your fleet with detailed aircraft profiles — registration, type, ICAO equipment codes, wake turbulence category, and emergency/survival equipment configuration.

📝

Custom Form Builder

Create your own form templates with sections and field types — text, textarea, select, checkbox. Save templates to the cloud and share with your team.

📷

QR Code Sharing

Generate QR codes for form links, flight plans, or custom URLs. Share instantly with ground crew, dispatch, or fellow pilots.

🔒

Offline Mode & Sync

Work offline with automatic data synchronization when you reconnect. Your forms, flights, and logbook entries are always safe with cloud backup.

🎓

Digital Pilot ID

Elegant glass-design digital pilot identification card. Store your license number, type, and medical certificate expiry — all accessible from your pocket.

👥

Team & Fleet Management

Manage crew members with role-based access, share aircraft fleets, and maintain audit trails. Perfect for flight schools and charter operators.
